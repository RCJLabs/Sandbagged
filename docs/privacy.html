Sandbagged — privacy

Sandbagged does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services.

What is stored, and where

Your saves, settings and records are written to your device's localStorage by the browser. They never leave the device. Clearing the site's data, or uninstalling the app, deletes them permanently — there is no copy anywhere else, and no way for anyone (including us) to recover one.

Network

The game is a single self-contained page. Once it has loaded it makes no network requests except to fetch its own files, which its service worker caches so the game works offline. Nothing is sent anywhere at any point.

Sharing

The share buttons hand a block of text or an image to your own device — the clipboard, or the system share sheet. Where that text or picture goes afterwards is entirely your choice, and is governed by whichever app you pass it to.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
